Data Confidentiality & Client Privacy

Privacy Policy

How personal information, contact numbers, and consultation requests are protected with strict confidentiality.

Effective Date: September 2026 • Applicable to all visitors and inquiry submissions

1 Commitment to Client Privacy

This Privacy Policy describes how Sanchita Adhikary Barman collects, handles, and safeguards the contact details and inquiries provided by prospective and existing policyholders. We respect your confidentiality and treat all personal interactions with the highest standards of integrity.

2 Information We Collect

When you submit a consultation request via our contact form or contact us via telephone or WhatsApp, we collect basic details necessary to serve you:

  • Full Name
  • 10-digit Mobile Phone Number (for call back or WhatsApp responses)
  • Email Address (for policy quotes and documentation)
  • Selected insurance requirement or service category
Strict Exemption: This public web portal does NOT collect or store sensitive financial credentials, net-banking passwords, UPI PINs, Credit Card CVVs, or full Aadhaar numbers.

3 How Your Information is Used

Collected information is utilized strictly for professional insurance advisory activities:

  • Responding directly to your queries and insurance planning needs
  • Preparing customized premium illustrations and benefit tables
  • Assisting with policy revival, nomination changes, or claim settlements
  • Dispatching requested LIC sales brochures or calculation tables

4 Zero Third-Party Marketing or Sale of Data

We uphold a strict zero-spam policy. Your personal contact details will never be sold, rented, leased, or traded to telemarketers, commercial advertisers, or third-party lead brokers. Data is communicated exclusively to the Life Insurance Corporation of India (LIC) when officially enrolling in a legitimate policy proposal upon your instruction.

5 Contact Regarding Privacy

If you wish to update, modify, or request the deletion of your inquiry records from our consultation logs, you may contact Sanchita Adhikary Barman directly via email at Sanchita.lic18@gmail.com or via phone at +91 7047870951.
