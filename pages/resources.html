Why Human Life Value (HLV) Matters

Most families are underinsured with basic policies of ₹2–5 Lakhs. HLV calculates true economic worth, ensuring your family can maintain their current lifestyle even in your absence.

The Cost of Delaying Savings

Starting a child education or retirement plan 5 years earlier can reduce the required monthly contribution by up to 40% due to the compounding effect of LIC guaranteed bonuses.

Sovereign Guarantee Security

Under Section 37 of the LIC Act, the Sum Assured and all accrued bonuses are 100% guaranteed by the Government of India, offering unmatched capital safety.
